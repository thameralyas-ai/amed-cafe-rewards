              VIP levels

              A customer reaches a level after this many visits, or after this many stamps in total if you set "Or stamps" (whichever comes first). Several stamps within 3 hours count as one visit.

              Special offers

              For example "Double stamps on Mondays". While an offer is on, each stamp counts extra.

              Events

              Match nights, live music and so on. Customers see upcoming events on their page, and staff can invite them on WhatsApp. Set stamps per purchase to 2 or more for bonus stamps during the event.

              Opening hours

              Stamps outside these hours need a second tap and show up under "Unusual activity" in Reports. A business day starts at opening time, so sales after midnight count for the same day.

              Customer page buttons
