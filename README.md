# Amed Cafe website and loyalty card

Served by GitHub Pages at **https://amedcaferest.com** (branch `main`, `CNAME`).

## Website (root)
- `index.html` is the public website ("Espresso & Gold" design) with an animated "Spotlight Stage" opening: the three shishas circle on a gold stage under a moving spotlight and the headline word changes (coffee, shisha, comfort); sections fade up as you scroll; phones set to Reduce Motion get a calmer version (half-speed turning, no spotlight sweep, fades instead of slides), as Thamir asked. Then: menu, rewards, offers and events, gallery and visit/contact, in Badini Kurdish, Arabic and English (flag switcher; Badini and Arabic are right-to-left).
- Visitors who have signed in to their loyalty card (localStorage `amed-session`) are sent straight to `/card/`, so old links, printed QR codes and old Home Screen icons keep opening the card. Add `?home` to the address to see the website anyway.
- `menu/index.html` is the QR menu (amedcaferest.com/menu/): the table QR codes open it. Menu only, straight away: sticky category bar, every category listed in order, prices in IQD, open/closed status, and a short loyalty-card and Google-review box at the end. Reads `../menu.json`, so it always matches the website menu. Same Espresso & Gold look and shared language choice (`amed-lang`); it does not redirect signed-in members to their card. The QR code points to `https://amedcaferest.com/menu/` (error correction H), so keep this address.
- New products: a menu category marked `"new": true` in `tools/build_menu.py` gets a "New" badge, appears in the website's "Our new products" section (above the menu, with "See in the menu" and, if listed in `FLYERS` in index.html, "See the flyer") and in a "New" link at the top of the QR menu. Categories can also have `banner` (wide photo) and `desc` (en/ar/ku). The first one is Asary fruit drinks (Hala.eco syrup, 9 flavours; with mineral water 1,500, sparkling water 2,000, milk 2,500), from Thamir's flyers in `media/new/` (`asary-flyer-1.jpg`, `asary-flyer-2.jpg`, `asary-bottles.jpg`). When it is no longer new, remove `"new":True` and the section disappears by itself.
- `menu.json` holds the menu: categories, items with names in `en`/`ar`/`ku`, prices in IQD, optional `options` (sizes/flavours) and `note`. It is generated by `python3 tools/build_menu.py menu.json`; edit the lists in that script, then rerun it.
- `tools/menu-source.json` is the raw copy of the menu taken from menu.instalacarte.com (9 Nov 2026). Amed Cafe no longer serves food, so only drinks and shisha are used. The site keeps separate English, Arabic and Kurdish menus whose prices and availability do not always match; the website uses the English section's prices and availability, with the Arabic and Kurdish names from the site. On 10 Nov 2026 Thamir confirmed these prices for every language: all mojitos (including Vimto) 2,000; all milkshakes 3,000; all lattes (including the iced Spanish latte) 3,000; Amed cocktail 5,000; half kilo of tobacco 20,000.
- Contact details (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, phone, Google Maps link, address in 3 languages) are in the `CONTACT` block at the top of the script in `index.html`. WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat show as a row of gold icons (an empty one is hidden); hours, location, phone and the Google review link are rows below it.
- Offers and events come from `data.json` (`offers`, `events`), so whatever the staff add in the staff tool appears automatically.
- Gallery ("The space") uses Thamir's own photos and video in `media/space/`: `inside.jpg` (the indoor seating, daytime), `shisha.jpg` (Amed shisha promo photo), `evening.jpg` (the seating at night with hanging plants) and `space.mp4` (10-second vertical clip: sign, barista, Christmas decorations; `space-poster.jpg` is its still). The video plays silently on a loop in the grid; tapping any tile opens it full screen (the video with sound). Originals were resized for the web. Use these as the reference for what the cafe looks like.

## Loyalty card (`/card/`)
- `card/index.html` is the customer card page (phone + PIN). Its footer links back to the website (`../?home`). It reads `../data.json` and the shared images (`../logo*.png`, `../shisha-*.png`).
- `card/sw.js` (service worker, scope `/card/`), `card/manifest*.webmanifest` and `card/icon-*.png` belong to the card. The service worker and the iPhone "Add to Home Screen" tip only run on `amedcaferest.com` / `github.io`.
- `sw.js` at the root is a retired worker that removes itself if an old copy is installed there.

## Shared files (keep at the root)
- `data.json` holds the cards, levels, offers and events. **Do not edit by hand**: it is regenerated hourly from the staff tool by `tools/sync.py` (run by the "Amed Cafe – hourly customer-page updater" routine). It contains no names or phone numbers: each card is stored under a key made from the customer's phone number and PIN. Do not move or rename `data.json` or `tools/sync.py` without updating that routine.
- `staff-guide.html` is the staff guide (linked from the staff tool).
- `logo.png`, `logo-gold.png`, `logo-silver.png`, `logo-copper.png` and `shisha-gold.png`, `shisha-silver.png`, `shisha-copper.png` are the brand logo and real photos of the cafe's shishas.
