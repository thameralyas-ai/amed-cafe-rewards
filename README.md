# Amed Cafe website and loyalty card

Served by GitHub Pages at **https://amedcaferest.com** (branch `main`, `CNAME`).

## Website (root)
- `index.html` is the public website ("Espresso & Gold" design) with an animated "Spotlight Stage" opening: the three shishas circle on a gold stage under a moving spotlight and the headline word changes (coffee, shisha, comfort); sections fade up as you scroll; phones set to Reduce Motion get a calmer version (half-speed turning, no spotlight sweep, fades instead of slides), as Thamir asked. Then: menu, rewards, offers and events, gallery and visit/contact, in Badini Kurdish, Arabic and English (flag switcher; Badini and Arabic are right-to-left).
- Visitors who have signed in to their loyalty card (localStorage `amed-session`) are sent straight to `/card/`, so old links, printed QR codes and old Home Screen icons keep opening the card. Add `?home` to the address to see the website anyway.
- `menu/index.html` is the QR menu (amedcaferest.com/menu/): the table QR codes open it. Menu only, straight away: sticky category bar, every category listed in order, prices in IQD, open/closed status, and a short loyalty-card and Google-review box at the end. Reads `../menu.json`, so it always matches the website menu. Same Espresso & Gold look and shared language choice (`amed-lang`); it does not redirect signed-in members to their card. The QR code points to `https://amedcaferest.com/menu/` (error correction H), so keep this address.
- New products: a menu category marked `"new": true` in `tools/build_menu.py` gets a "New" badge, appears in the website's "Our new products" section (above the menu, with "See in the menu" and, if listed in `FLYERS` in index.html, "See the flyer") and in a "New" link at the top of the QR menu. Categories can also have `banner` (wide photo), `desc` (en/ar/ku) and `flavours` (name + picture, shown as tiles; Asary's fruit pictures are cut from the flyer, in `media/new/flavours/`). The first one is Asary fruit drinks (Hala.eco syrup, 9 flavours; with mineral water 1,500, sparkling water 2,000, milk 2,500), from Thamir's flyers in `media/new/` (`asary-flyer-1.jpg`, `asary-flyer-2.jpg`). The banner `asary-bottles-real.jpg` is made from the real bottle photos Thamir sent (Hala.eco product photos, 10 Nov 2026); there was no Peach photo, so the Peach bottle is the Mango photo with its label changed to "TASTE OF PEACH" / "طـعم الخوخ" (Rubik and IBM Plex Sans Arabic fonts, matched to the label). Thamir chose to keep the fruit pictures for the flavour tiles. 
- Drink photos (11 Nov 2026, from Amed's own Instagram @amed.caferest via the Composio connector; text and logos cropped or painted out): `media/drinks/`. They are shown as a "From our bar" swipe row (`gallery` in `tools/build_menu.py` -> `menu.json`; `#bargal` / `.bar*` in index.html above the menu tabs and in menu/index.html above the categories), not next to single items, so it does not look like only a few drinks have photos (Thamir's wish). Tapping a photo opens its menu section. Names start with "bar" because the website already uses `#gal`/`galT` for "The space".
- The shisha section now shows Amed's real shisha photo (`media/drinks/shisha-photo.jpg`, cropped so the customer is not shown, background person blurred) with the smoke starting at `smoke: [66, 13]` (% of the photo); JPG photos get the `.shot.ph` style.
- Items can have `{"popular": True}` (5th field in build_menu.py) for a "Best seller" badge: Pistachio coffee (Thamir's best seller). Coffee descriptions are based on Amed's Instagram coffee post. Amed latte is a house secret on purpose ("Our house secret. Ask us about it"): never describe what is in it.
- 11 Nov 2026: Bolo (lemon & mint juice) and Gazoz removed; Pistachio coffee (single 1,500 / double 3,000, confirmed by Thamir) and Blueberry mojito (2,000) added. When it is no longer new, remove `"new":True` and the section disappears by itself.
- `tools/qr/`: `qr-menu.svg` is the QR code for https://amedcaferest.com/menu/ (made with segno, error correction H; it only holds the address, so menu changes never need a new QR). `cards.html` has the 3 sample table cards (A6), `samples.png` shows them side by side. Thamir chose card 1 (Espresso & Gold, 10 Nov 2026): print files `amed-table-card-A6.pdf` (one card, 105 x 148 mm) and `amed-table-cards-A4-x4.pdf` (4 cards on A4 with cut lines), made from `cards.html` with Playwright `page.pdf`; the QR was checked with a QR reader.
- Motion layer (`js/motion.js`, libraries in `vendor/`): GSAP 3.15.1 + ScrollTrigger and Lenis 1.3.27 (smooth wheel scrolling, run from GSAP's ticker so ScrollTrigger and Lenis share each frame), plus vanilla ports of four React Bits effects (the site has no React): Blur Text on the homepage headline (word by word, so Arabic stays joined), Star Border on the "My loyalty card" button, and Count Up on the rewards numbers. Sections fade up with ScrollTrigger. With Reduce Motion: no smooth scroll, fades only, numbers shown at once, rays slowed, star border still. React Bits is MIT + Commons Clause (fine to use in this website; do not sell or redistribute the components); the notice is in `js/motion.js`.
- Navigation: a round ☰ button stays at the top corner of the homepage (left in Arabic/Badini) and opens a full-screen list: The menu (opens the QR menu page `menu/`, same as scanning the QR), Our new products (only while there is one), Amed rewards, Offers & events, The space, Find us, and a My loyalty card button. Thamir chose this (option 3).
- Under the spinning shishas, the label alternates between the two flavours these shishas are served with, English mix and Royal mix (`STAGE_FLAVOURS` in index.html; names come from the menu in each language), changing each time a new shisha turns to the front.
- Spotlight Stage ring light: a short dash moving along an SVG oval drawn on the ring (`.ring svg`, CSS stroke-dashoffset). Do not go back to spinning a CSS shape with a blur filter: on iPhone that drew a stray dotted line through the stage. The Light Rays canvas and the old spotlight beam were removed from the hero for the same reason (still gold gradient instead); `lightRays()` is still in `js/motion.js` but not used.
- `menu.json` holds the menu: categories, items with names in `en`/`ar`/`ku`, prices in IQD, optional `options` (sizes/flavours) and `note`. It is generated by `python3 tools/build_menu.py menu.json`; edit the lists in that script, then rerun it.
- `tools/menu-source.json` is the raw copy of the menu taken from menu.instalacarte.com (9 Nov 2026). Amed Cafe no longer serves food, so only drinks and shisha are used. The site keeps separate English, Arabic and Kurdish menus whose prices and availability do not always match; the website uses the English section's prices and availability, with the Arabic and Kurdish names from the site. On 10 Nov 2026 Thamir confirmed these prices for every language: all mojitos (including Vimto) 2,000; all milkshakes 3,000; all lattes (including the iced Spanish latte) 3,000; Amed cocktail 5,000; half kilo of tobacco 20,000. On 10 Nov 2026 Thamir cut the shisha list to only what is served now: gum & mint, lemon & mint, double apple, Royal mix, English mix (all 5,000), head change 3,000, half kilo of tobacco 20,000, 1 kilo of tobacco 40,000. The same day he removed smoothies from the menu. The shisha photo in the shisha section (website and QR menu) has soft puffs of smoke rising from the bowl (Thamir chose "Soft puffs" of 3 samples; `.smoke` CSS and `shot()` in index.html and menu/index.html; slower for Reduce Motion). Menu items (website and QR menu): on mouse hover, or while a finger is on an item on a phone, a soft gold glow with a gold bar at the start appears and the name turns bold gold with the price slightly bigger (Thamir chose samples 1 + 3 together; `.li` hover CSS in both pages).
- Contact details (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, phone, Google Maps link, address in 3 languages) are in the `CONTACT` block at the top of the script in `index.html`. WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok and Snapchat show as a row of gold icons (an empty one is hidden); hours, location, phone and the Google review link are rows below it.
- Offers and events come from `data.json` (`offers`, `events`), so whatever the staff add in the staff tool appears automatically.
- Gallery ("The space") is a Circular Carousel (`js/carousel.js`, vanilla port of React Bits CircularCarousel, cylinder with flat cards; Thamir asked for it): Thamir's photos and video in `media/space/` (`inside.jpg`, `shisha.jpg`, `evening.jpg`, `space.mp4` with `space-poster.jpg`, plus stills from that video: `sign.jpg`, `bar.jpg`, `drink.jpg`; the Christmas frames were left out). Photos are black and white; colour comes back on mouse hover (computer) or after a tap (phone). Tapping/clicking a photo brings it to the front; tapping the front photo again opens it full screen (the video with sound). It turns slowly by itself, can be dragged/swiped, and stops turning with Reduce Motion. Use these photos as the reference for what the cafe looks like.

## Loyalty card (`/card/`)
- `card/index.html` is the customer card page (phone + PIN). Its footer links back to the website (`../?home`). It reads `../data.json` and the shared images (`../logo*.png`, `../shisha-*.png`).
- `card/sw.js` (service worker, scope `/card/`), `card/manifest*.webmanifest` and `card/icon-*.png` belong to the card. The service worker and the iPhone "Add to Home Screen" tip only run on `amedcaferest.com` / `github.io`.
- `sw.js` at the root is a retired worker that removes itself if an old copy is installed there.

## Shared files (keep at the root)
- `data.json` holds the cards, levels, offers and events. **Do not edit by hand**: it is regenerated hourly from the staff tool by `tools/sync.py` (run by the "Amed Cafe – hourly customer-page updater" routine). It contains no names or phone numbers: each card is stored under a key made from the customer's phone number and PIN. Do not move or rename `data.json` or `tools/sync.py` without updating that routine.
- `staff-guide.html` is the staff guide (linked from the staff tool).
- `logo.png`, `logo-gold.png`, `logo-silver.png`, `logo-copper.png` and `shisha-gold.png`, `shisha-silver.png`, `shisha-copper.png` are the brand logo and real photos of the cafe's shishas.
