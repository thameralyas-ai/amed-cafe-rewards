# Amed Cafe Rewards

The customer page for the Amed Cafe loyalty card. Customers enter their phone number and PIN to see their drink and shisha stamps, free rewards, VIP level and visits, in Kurdish, Arabic or English.

- `index.html` is the page.
- `data.json` holds the cards. It contains no names or phone numbers: each card is stored under a key made from the customer's phone number and PIN, so a card can only be found with both.
- `logo.png` is the Amed Cafe logo.

The staff manage members in the Amed Cafe staff tool; `data.json` is regenerated from it.
